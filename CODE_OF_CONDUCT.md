# Code of conduct

Gemma Grounded Health is a place to build careful, honest open models and evaluations together. We want everyone who
takes part, in issues, pull requests, discussions or any other project space, to be treated
with respect, whatever their background, identity or level of experience.

## What we expect

- Be kind and assume good intent. Critique ideas and code, not people.
- Be precise and honest, especially about results: say what the evidence shows and what it
  does not.
- Welcome newcomers and questions; explain context rather than dismissing.
- Accept a maintainer's decision on scope gracefully, and disagree openly but civilly.

## What is not acceptable

Harassment, insults or personal attacks; discriminatory jokes or language; sexualised
language or imagery; publishing someone's private information; sustained disruption; and
encouraging any of these.

## Enforcement

Report a problem privately through the repository's
[private vulnerability reporting](https://github.com/biplob819/gemma-grounded-health/security/advisories/new)
form (choose a clear title such as "conduct report"), or by contacting the maintainer
listed on the GitHub profile. Reports are handled confidentially. Maintainers may edit or
remove contributions, and warn, suspend or ban participants, as the situation warrants.

This code is inspired by the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/),
version 2.1.
