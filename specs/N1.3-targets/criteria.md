# N1.3 acceptance criteria

**TGT-01** — The recommendation filter drops every sentence matching the pinned patterns (`should`, `recommend`, `must`, `we suggest`, `clinicians`, `further stud`, `warrant`, `need to`, `advis`); a test lists each pattern with a matching and a non-matching sentence.
**TGT-02** — `support(sentence, cited)` is the share of the sentence's content words present in the cited sentences' content words; content words, stopwords and stemming are pinned with tests; a sentence under 0.5 is dropped.
**TGT-03** — The NLI filter keeps a sentence only when entailment ≥ 0.5 with exactly its cited sentences as premise; label indices come from the model's `id2label`; the model id and revision are recorded in the manifest; tests use an injected scorer and never download a model.
**TGT-04** — Citation targets follow TGT-R6 (the smallest of the top 1–4 non-zero-overlap sentences that passes support and entailment, D-35; merged, sorted, ≤ 4, within 1..n) after the TGT-R9 hedge strip; a sentence whose best overlap is zero is dropped, never cited.
**TGT-05** — Answers over 80 words and rows with no surviving sentence are dropped and counted by reason in `drop_report.json`.
**TGT-06** — The mix holds (OWNER D-34): answer yes:no 60:40, insufficient 10% and off-scope 1% of each split (each within one example of exact); train ≤ 20,000 and as large as the surviving "no" rows allow; val is one tenth of train by the same rules; the shortfall is recorded; every row validates as `TrainExample`; train and val share no PMID.
**TGT-07** — Insufficient examples remove every RESULTS/CONCLUSIONS-labelled and every cited sentence, renumber, keep ≥ 2 sentences, use one of the 4 fixed answers and cite the remaining sentences most similar to the question.
**TGT-08** — Off-scope examples (1% of each split, OWNER D-34) use 12 question and 6 answer templates; no 5-gram of a training template occurs in `data/offscope_eval.jsonl`.
**TGT-09** — Every train and val row is reproduced exactly by `reconstruct(source_row, rule_ids)` from its N1.2 source row; no other text source exists in the targets code.
**TGT-10** — The local audit page shows 50 seeded, stratified examples one at a time, binds each label to a digest of the shown text, autosaves, and `ggh audit score` reports pass only when ≥ 45 are `ok` and no answer example is `unsupported_answer`; the owner's audit result is recorded in RUN-STATE.md.
