# P5.1 — Write-up

**Wave 5 (publish). Depends on:** R4.5.

## Goal
Tell the story of the project for a reader who will not open the code: how it was built, what it found (including the v1 failure and what v2 fixed and did not), with every number generated from the results bundle, never typed (PRD deliverables, D-20).

## Design
- **How I built it (PUB-01).** `docs/writeup/how-i-built-it.md`: the harness, data and targets, training on Kaggle, export and the laptop, evaluation on EvalKit, the v1 grounding failure and the v2 retrain, the release; honest about misses (D-55), the aim rule (D-62), model reviews (D-60, D-61) and the local demo (D-63).
- **Project page text (PUB-02).** `docs/writeup/project-page.md`, generated by `ggh writeup` from the bundle and committed evidence; a test checks it equals the generator and that every number traces to the bundle.
- **Decisions (PUB-03).** `docs/writeup/decisions-summary.md`: the decisions that shaped the result, each one line, linking `docs/decisions.md` and a one-page PRD summary.
- **Video script (PUB-04).** `docs/writeup/video-script.md`: about 90 seconds, including the offline laptop segment (the local demo on the laptop, the GGUF through llama-server or Ollama).
