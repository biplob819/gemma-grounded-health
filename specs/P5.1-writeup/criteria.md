# P5.1 criteria

**PUB-01** — `docs/writeup/how-i-built-it.md` covers each stage from data to release, the v1 failure and the v2 result, and states every miss and caveat the decisions record (D-55, D-60–D-63); every number in it traces to the bundle or a committed file.
**PUB-02** — `docs/writeup/project-page.md` is generated from the bundle by a command, equals its output, and contains no typed number.
**PUB-03** — `docs/writeup/decisions-summary.md` summarises the decisions that shaped the result with ids that exist in `docs/decisions.md`, and links the PRD.
**PUB-04** — `docs/writeup/video-script.md` is a timed script of about 90 seconds that includes the offline laptop segment, and claims nothing the bundle or the cards do not.
